नियम व शर्तें
  1. Respectful Conduct: All residents shall maintain discipline, dignity and courteous behaviour. Ragging, harassment, bullying or misconduct is strictly prohibited.
  2. Timely Payment of Fees: Monthly accommodation fee and maintenance charge must be paid between the 1st and 10th of every month. Late fee ₹50/day after due date.
  3. Maintenance Charge: A monthly maintenance charge of ₹200 applies in addition to the hostel fee.
  4. Cleanliness & Hygiene: Keep rooms and common areas clean; dispose of waste only in designated bins.
  5. Care of Property: Hostel property, furniture and appliances must be used with care. Damage due to negligence will be recovered from the resident.
  6. Personal Belongings: Residents are responsible for their own valuables; management is not liable for loss or theft.
  7. Entry & Exit Register: Mandatory to record entry/exit at the gate.
  8. Gate Timings: Gate closes at 9:30 PM. Late return needs prior permission and parent/guardian info.
  9. Visitors: Allowed only during permitted hours in designated areas. Male visitors not allowed in rooms.
  10. Prohibited Substances: Alcohol, narcotics, tobacco or any illegal substance is strictly forbidden on premises.
  11. Electrical & Fire Safety: No unauthorised high-power appliances, unsafe wiring or candles. Switch off appliances when leaving the room.
  12. Noise & Disturbance: Loud music or activity disturbing other residents is prohibited, especially during quiet hours.
  13. Room Occupancy: Occupy only the allotted room/bed. Subletting is not permitted without written approval.
  14. Alterations: No drilling, painting or structural/electrical changes without permission.
  15. Safety & Emergencies: Report any security concern, accident, medical emergency or hazard to management immediately.
  16. Vacating the Room: Give at least 30 days' notice before vacating, otherwise the security deposit will not be refunded.
  17. Compliance: Management may take disciplinary action, including termination of stay, for rule violations.
  18. Final Authority: Management's decision on all matters of boarding, residence and discipline is final and binding.
